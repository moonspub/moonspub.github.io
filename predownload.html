Custom Weapons Predownload

This page contains a link to the predownload for our custom weapons content. Downloading our assets from here will be much quicker than downloading it while connecting. This will take several minutes as opposed to several hours.

Download Assets

Installation

You'll need to extract the contents of this file to your game's download folder. Not your game's install files.

  1. Right click Team Fortress 2 Classified in your Steam library
  2. Click "Properties"
  3. Click "Installed Files"
  4. Click "Browse"
  5. Go into the tf2classified folder
  6. The folder you're looking for is named "downloads"

Next copy the materials, models, particles, scenes, and sound folders into this downloads folder. If your operating system asks you if you want to merge folders, accept.

Restart your game and try connecting. A few files will still need to download, but it will skip a vast majority of them.

Questions and Answers

Will I need to redownload this each update?

No. Your game will automatically fetch new files. This just makes the initial download quicker.

Why is my game downloading things so slowly/why do I need to do this?

The Source engine uses a system called FastDL for downloading content when connecting. Despite its name, it's slow for many small files. Your internet connection and the server isn't slow, it's just that FastDL isn't designed for custom weapon content.

Can I directly merge the folders (materials, models...) with the ones in tf2classified?

No. Do not do this. This will mess up future downloads and cause issues. Instead, put it in the "downloads" subdirectory under tf2classified.
